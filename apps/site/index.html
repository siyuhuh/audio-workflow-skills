01

歌曲包The package

一首歌，一个文件夹。One song, one folder.

做好的歌曲包留在你的硬盘上，只有四样东西。

A finished package stays on your disk and holds four things.

  • 01

    MV

    画面。演唱时静音，声音交给伴奏。

    The picture. It is muted while you sing, and the backing track carries the sound.

  • 02

    原唱Original

    完整的原曲，随时切回来听。

    The full song. Switch back to it whenever you want to hear it.

  • 03

    伴奏Backing track

    在本机做人声分离得到的伴奏。

    Made on your Mac by separating the vocals from the song.

  • 04

    歌词Lyrics

    一份带时间的歌词文件（LRC），采用前先和本地识别比对。

    One timed lyric file (LRC), checked against local recognition before use.

每个文件夹带一份 manifest.json 清单。

Each folder carries a manifest.json that lists its contents.

02

流程How it works

加歌，处理，开唱。Add, process, sing.

  1. 1

    加一首歌Add a song

    粘贴链接，搜索 YouTube 或哔哩哔哩，或者选一个本地音视频文件。

    Paste a link, search YouTube or Bilibili, or pick a local audio or video file.

  2. 2

    在本机处理Process on your Mac

    人声分离和歌词识别都在你的 Mac 上完成。被中断的任务会标记为失败并留在队列里，等你处理，不会自己重跑。

    Vocal separation and lyric recognition both run on your Mac. An interrupted job is marked failed and stays in the queue for you; it does not restart on its own.

  3. 3

    进 Room 开唱Sing in the Room

    排好点歌队列，在原唱和伴奏之间切换，然后开唱、录音、混音。队列在重启后还在。

    Line up the queue, switch between original and backing, then sing, record and mix. The queue survives a restart.

03

安装Install

下载即用。Download and it works.

  • 双击就能打开Opens with a double-click

    使用 Developer ID 签名并通过 Apple 公证，不用右键“打开”，也不用去“隐私与安全性”里放行。

    Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. No right-click → Open, and no trip to Privacy & Security.

  • 不用再装别的Nothing else to install

    安装包自带运行时和默认模型：Whisper small（约 484 MB）和 UVR 人声分离（约 67 MB）。不需要 Python、Homebrew 或 HF Token。

    The installer bundles the runtime and the default models: Whisper small (about 484 MB) and the UVR vocal separator (about 67 MB). No Python, Homebrew or HF token needed.

  • 本地文件可以离线Local files work offline

    处理本地文件、播放歌曲包和录音都可以离线完成。在线搜索和下载需要网络。

    Processing local files, playing packages and recording all work offline. Online search and downloads need a network.
